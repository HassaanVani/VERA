A two-dimensional linear transformation is represented by a 2 × 2 matrix A. Its first column is the image of (1, 0); its second column is the image of (0, 1). A matrix-vector product transforms coordinates.

A nonzero vector v is an eigenvector with eigenvalue λ when Av = λv. Find eigenvalues using det(A − λI) = 0. For a 2 × 2 matrix [[a,b],[c,d]], the trace is a + d and determinant is ad − bc. Distinct real eigenvalues have independent eigendirections.

Application vocabulary: a calibration probe is simply an input vector. Its recorded output is the same matrix applied to that vector. Coordinates in this packet are dimensionless.
